## Recovery and reproducibility

Evidence and inference remain separate. **Evidence:** canonical archive bytes, retained report objects, source-hashed raw rows/projections, embedded native case dictionaries, dated owner/process observations and copy receipts. **Inference:** those sources permit bounded descriptive claims for their actual populations and constrain which further reanalyses are possible. **Unknown:** final C06 outcome, full V799 raw whereabouts, unsearched backups and unavailable retained-session contents.

The four C06 dictionaries are physically retained in [recovered-c06-observations.json](smoke/recovered-c06-observations.json), with exact source JSON locators and deduplication by caseId. Their flow duration, elapsed wrapper duration and native spans are distinct boundaries. They are recovered historical measurements, not reruns or reconstructed values. The V485/V497 packet preserves each original inspected member's bytes/SHA and timing fields; it is a selective projection, not a complete raw reconstruction.

Saved-data procedures can recompute retained measurements. They cannot recreate unretained historical samples, unobserved timeouts, physical acoustics, omitted device states or final denominators. A future repeat is a new cohort, never recovery of the original run. No forced900 repeat is authorized or required by this audit.

The owner's clarification controls acceptance: per-request proof artifacts are desirable, not an automatic universal methodology gate. Missing optional artifacts require claim-specific limitations. Existing measured cohorts retain their reported/recomputed evidence, honest uncertainty and failures; no blanket invalidation or universal rerun demand follows.
